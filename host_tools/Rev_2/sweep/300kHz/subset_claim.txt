   all eight      8    0.09946       0.017387    3.1215
   ADFH           4    0.00128       0.000003    1.5805

   Removing the coupling saves 0.017384 bit/sample of mutual information,
   which is 0.56% of the full budget (3.1215 bit/sample).
   It gives up 1.5410 bit/sample of entropy, 49.4% of the budget.
   Trade ratio: 89 bits of entropy given up per bit of mutual information removed.
   Note both figures rest on per-channel H measured with one ring enabled at a time;
   whether four rings together really deliver their sum is exactly what part 5 tests.

5. THE CONTROL EXPERIMENT THAT WOULD SETTLE IT (needs the board)
   Compare a clean subset (ADFH) with a deliberately coupled one (BCEF,
   which contains two adjacent pairs) at the same ring count, then check each
   assessed joint entropy against the sum of its channels. If coupling costs
   real entropy, the coupled subset falls further below its sum.

   # subset ADFH
   python3 usb_read.py -s '!a!B!C!d!E!f!G!h!r' -o channelADFH.bin
   python3 unpack_single_channel.py channelADFH.bin outputADFH.bin --bits 4 --bit-order little
   ea_non_iid -v -i outputADFH.bin 4
   # sum of per-channel H for ADFH: 1.5805 bit/sample

   # subset BCEF
   python3 usb_read.py -s '!A!b!c!D!e!f!G!H!r' -o channelBCEF.bin
   python3 unpack_single_channel.py channelBCEF.bin outputBCEF.bin --bits 4 --bit-order little
   ea_non_iid -v -i outputBCEF.bin 4
   # sum of per-channel H for BCEF: 1.4734 bit/sample

   Read it as: H_original(subset) versus that sum. Rev-1 saw the joint
   figure come out far ABOVE the sum, which was a sign the joint
   assessment is optimistic on interleaved sources - so treat a small
   gap as inconclusive and a large deficit as real.
